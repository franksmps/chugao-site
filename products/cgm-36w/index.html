CGM-36W — LED Adapters (5-200W)

36W AC/DC LED adapter, 12V / 24V / 36V output options.

Overview

The CGM-36W adapts 100-240V mains to 12V, 24V or 36V DC for higher-power LED strips and modules. Four protections built in, local plug fitted per market, CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 36W
  • Output: DC 12V / 24V / 36V
  • Ingress protection: IP20
  • Input: AC 100-240V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
