CGF-400W — Drivers LED impermeáveis IP67 (10-400W)

400W IP67 waterproof LED driver, 12V / 24V, fully potted.

Visão geral

The CGF-400W is the 400W top of the waterproof range for 12V or 24V outdoor LEDs. Fully potted IP67/IP68, salt-spray tested, 90-305V input, CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 400W
  • Saída: DC 12V / 24V
  • Grau de proteção: IP67 / IP68
  • Entrada: AC 90-305V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
