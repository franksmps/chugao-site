C-200W — Внутренние драйверы LED (50-400 Вт)

200W indoor LED driver, 12V / 24V, built-in active PFC.

Обзор

The C-200W delivers 200W for 12V or 24V indoor LED fixtures with active PFC and fan-less operation. Четыре вида защиты built in, CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 200W
  • Выход: DC 12V / 24V
  • Степень защиты: IP20
  • Вход: AC 190-264V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
