CGF-200W — IP67 防水 LED 驱动电源（10-400W）

200W IP67 waterproof LED driver, 12V / 24V, fully potted.

概览

The CGF-200W is a 200W fully potted IP67/IP68 waterproof driver for 12V or 24V outdoor LEDs. Silicone potting blocks water and salt spray; 90-305V input covers most sites. CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 200W
  • 输出： DC 12V / 24V
  • 防护等级: IP67 / IP68
  • 输入： AC 90-305V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
