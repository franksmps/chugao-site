LED Power Supply Catalog

One factory, four product lines, covering most low-voltage LED jobs from a short strip behind a shelf to a weatherproof sign outside a shop. Every unit passes a 48-hour full-load burn-in and ships CE and RoHS as standard. Pick a line below, or tell us your load and we will route you to the right model.

LED Adapters 5-200W

Compact 12V/24V/36V/48V AC-DC adapters for strips, modules and signage. Universal 100-240V input, one SKU ships anywhere.

Indoor Drivers 50-400W

Constant-voltage drivers with active PFC and flicker-free output for ceilings, panels and downlights.

IP67 Waterproof 10-400W

Fully potted, salt-spray tested drivers for fountains, coastal and wash-down sites.

IP65 Rainproof 100-600W

Vented metal-case drivers for signage and semi-outdoor installs up to 600W.
