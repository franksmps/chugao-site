CGF-100W — مشغلات LED ضد الماء IP67 (10-400 واط)

100W IP67 waterproof LED driver, 12V / 24V, fully potted.

نظرة عامة

The CGF-100W is a 100W fully potted IP67/IP68 waterproof driver for 12V or 24V outdoor LEDs. Silicone potting blocks water and salt spray; 90-305V input covers most sites. CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 100W
  • الخرج: DC 12V / 24V
  • حماية الدخول: IP67 / IP68
  • الدخل: AC 90-305V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
