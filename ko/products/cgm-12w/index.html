CGM-12W — LED 어댑터 (5-200W)

12W AC/DC LED adapter, 12V output for LED strips and modules.

개요

The CGM-12W is a compact 12W AC/DC adapter for 12V LED strips, LED modules and small fixtures. The universal 100-240V input means one SKU ships worldwide; we fit the local plug for your market at no extra charge. CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 12W
  • 출력: DC 12V
  • 방수/방진 등급: IP20
  • 입력: AC 100-240V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
