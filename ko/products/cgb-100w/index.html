CGB-100W — 실내용 LED 드라이버 (50-400W)

CGB-100W indoor LED driver, 12V / 24V, dimming ready.

개요

The CGB-100W is a 100W indoor driver for 12V or 24V fixtures with dimming support (0-10V / PWM on selected models). Active PFC, fan-less, four protections, CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 100W
  • 출력: DC 12V / 24V
  • 방수/방진 등급: IP20
  • 입력: AC 190-264V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
