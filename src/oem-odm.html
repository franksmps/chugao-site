LED Driver OEM & ODM

CHUGAO builds custom LED drivers and AC/DC adapters to your specification — not just a logo on a stock unit. Send us the electrical and mechanical drawing and our engineering team returns a quote, a sample lead time, and the certification plan for your target market. We already build private-label drivers for brands in Germany, Brazil and Saudi Arabia.

What we can customize

  • Electrical: output voltage (12V/24V/36V/48V), current, constant voltage or constant current, active PFC, flicker-free ripple.
  • Mechanical: enclosure size and shape, vented or fully potted, mounting feet or tabs.
  • Interface: connector type, cable length and gauge, DC plug or terminal block.
  • Control: 0-10V, PWM, DALI or TRIAC dimming; wide input range for global markets.
  • Branding: silkscreen or laser marking on the housing, your label artwork and barcodes, printed cartons.

The process

  1. Send the spec. A datasheet, drawing or even a competitor model number — we will quote an equivalent.
  2. Sample & verify. Prototypes are built and tested on the same routine as mass production, so what you approve is what ships.
  3. Certification plan. CE/RoHS as standard; UL per model for North America; BIS for India on request. Reports ship with the batch.
  4. Mass production. 48-hour full-load burn-in on every unit, then export cartons with commercial invoice, packing list and certificate of origin.
