CGB-200W — Indoor LED Drivers (50-400W)

CGB-200W indoor LED driver, 12V / 24V, dimming ready.

Overview

The CGB-200W is a 200W indoor driver for 12V or 24V fixtures with dimming support (0-10V / PWM on selected models). Active PFC, fan-less, four protections, CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 200W
  • Output: DC 12V / 24V
  • Ingress protection: IP20
  • Input: AC 190-264V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
