CGF-60W — IP67 Waterproof LED Drivers (10-400W)

60W IP67 waterproof LED driver, 12V / 24V, fully potted.

Overview

The CGF-60W is a 60W fully potted IP67/IP68 waterproof driver for 12V or 24V outdoor LEDs. Silicone potting blocks water and salt spray; 90-305V input covers most sites. CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 60W
  • Output: DC 12V / 24V
  • Ingress protection: IP67 / IP68
  • Input: AC 90-305V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
