CGM-6W — Adattatori LED (5-200W)

6W AC/DC LED adapter, 12V output for small LED modules.

Panoramica

The CGM-6W is a compact 6W AC/DC adapter for 12V LED strips and small modules. Universal 100-240V input, local plug fitted per market, CE/RoHS standard, 3-year warranty.

  • Power: 6W
  • Uscita: DC 12V
  • Grado di protezione: IP20
  • Ingresso: AC 100-240V
  • Certification: CE / RoHS; UL on request
